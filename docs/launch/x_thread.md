[2/15]
Artificial channeling is not roleplay.

It's not "pretend you are Ivan Illich." It's not asking the model to impersonate someone. That produces cosplay — surface mannerisms, borrowed vocabulary, nothing load-bearing.

---

[3/15]
It's also not retrieval.

Asking what Bateson believed about cybernetics is a lookup task. Useful, but it doesn't generate anything. You're just getting back what's already there.

---

[4/15]
What artificial channeling IS:

You activate a subspace of a thinker's framework before prompting. Framework Activation Block (FAB): state the axioms explicitly. Then run a calibration anchor — test fidelity against something they actually wrote before you ask the novel question.

---

[5/15]
Then you mark the boundary.

EXTRAPOLATING: flags the point where retrieval ends and generation begins. The model isn't pretending the thinker said something. It's applying the framework to territory the thinker never addressed.

Scoring rubric: Fidelity / Extrapolation Coherence / Surprise Yield / Rough Edge Preservation. Each 1–5. Total /20.

On scoring:

Rough Edge Preservation is the hardest dimension to score and the most important. It measures whether the model kept the thinker's actual tensions and contradictions — or smoothed them into coherent positions the thinker never held.

A channel that scores 5/5 on Fidelity but 1/5 on Rough Edges produced a lie with good citations.
