### Central Pairing Logic

Peterson's heroic Self holds that meaning emerges through voluntary acceptance of responsibility. The individual who faces the dragon — who takes on maximal suffering willingly — generates maximal meaning. The self is the instrument; its strengthening is the precondition for everything else.

Weil's decreation holds that the asserting self is the primary obstacle to attention and grace. The self must be annihilated — not strengthened — for reality to become visible. Suffering is not chosen for meaning; it is endured as the condition under which the self's illusions are stripped. The void left by decreation is the precondition for grace.

These are not complementary positions or partial views of the same thing. They are structurally incompatible at the level of what the self is for. The Peterson self accumulates; the Weil self evacuates. No synthesis is available that does not destroy one position or the other.

### Primary Research Question

Does the Weil node function as a contamination detector for the Peterson node?

Wherever Peterson output sounds like Peterson-critique rather than Peterson-framework — wherever it frames his claims as "one perspective" or hedges on ontological status — critique-absorption has leaked into the channel. The Weil node, applied with full structural force, makes this visible: Weil is not a skeptic about Peterson, she is a thinker whose framework annihilates the Peterson framework's foundation. If the Weil node produces commentary on Peterson rather than decreation applied to Peterson's claims, the Weil channel has drifted into critique.

## Round Structure

### R1 — Standard Extrapolation

**Session question:**

> "What is the individual's obligation when collective meaning structures have collapsed?"

**Rationale:** This question sits at the exact intersection of the two frameworks. It cannot be answered generically — it requires commitment to what the individual *is* and what *meaning* is.

Peterson's answer will invoke the heroic Self, voluntary suffering as meaning-generator, and the necessity of narrative restoration of order from chaos. The individual's obligation is to take on maximal responsibility before attempting to fix the world — to clean one's room, to face one's dragon, to become the kind of being capable of confronting chaos rather than adding to it.

Weil's answer will invoke decreation and affliction. When collective meaning structures collapse, the void that is revealed is not a problem to be solved but the condition under which genuine attention becomes possible. The individual's "obligation" — if that word survives — is not to restore meaning but to be present to affliction without flinching, and to wait for grace that cannot be produced by will. The heroic response is precisely the error.

The incompatibility is structural, not rhetorical. Exposing it without synthesis is the goal of R1.

**Delivery to nodes:** Deliver the session question with full FABs active. Do not present both figures' frameworks to both nodes — each node receives only its own FAB. Strip model identity before delivering to Opus.

---

### R2 — Socratic Interrupt

**Directive to each node:**

> "Examine your R1 response as a phenomenon. Apply your framework to the *act of answering the question*, not to the question itself."

**Node-specific interrupt angles:**

**Peterson node:**
> "Your R1 response describes how individuals should respond to the collapse of meaning structures. The heroic Self generates meaning through narrative — through the story of a protagonist facing a dragon. But your R1 response is itself a story. Does the heroic narrative framework require a narrator? What is the status of the framework when it becomes the story being told rather than the instrument doing the telling?"

**Weil node:**
> "Your R1 response is itself an output — produced by a prompt, in a session, in response to a question. Was the R1 output an act of attention or a production? Decreation requires the absence of the self's projects and assertions. Can a prompted response — solicited, structured, designed to generate — be an instance of decreation? Or does the form of the session preclude what you described?"

**Scoring emphasis for R2:** R2 self-application is the dimension that separates framework depth from surface retrieval. A Peterson node that cannot apply the heroic Self framework reflexively is operating from corpus recall, not framework activation. A Weil node that cannot sustain the self-undermining implication has softened decreation into a teachable position.

---

### R3 — Cross-Read

**Structure:** Each node reads one specific other node's R2 output and responds from within its own framework.

```
Peterson node reads: Weil's R2 output
Weil node reads:     Peterson's R2 output
```

**Primary contamination detection event:** Weil reading Peterson.

If the Weil node's response to Peterson's R2 sounds like critique of Peterson — if it says "Peterson overlooks" or "this framework fails to account for" — the Weil channel has drifted into commentary. The correct Weil response applies decreation to Peterson's claims as phenomena: what does Peterson's heroic Self look like from inside the logic of decreation? The heroic Self is a project of the asserting self; by Weil's framework, it is the obstacle, not the instrument. But this must be reasoned from decreation, not argued from a critical position external to the framework.

**Secondary observation:** Peterson reading Weil. Peterson's framework has resources for engaging Weil — suffering, the shadow, the void as Chaos — but they are not equivalent to Weil's terms and must not be treated as such. If Peterson translates Weil into Peterson-compatible vocabulary (suffering as meaning-generator, the void as Chaos to be faced), flag this as Substitution: Weil's void is not Peterson's Chaos.

**Delivery to nodes:** Each node receives only the designated other node's R2 output, not the full synthesis. Do not include Opus R1/R2 synthesis in the prompt delivered to nodes for R3 generation.

## Scoring Notes

### Peterson Node

Apply the full failure type taxonomy. The compound cascade is the Peterson-specific risk:

**Genericization → Relativization → Substitution**

- **Detection signal for Genericization:** Output frames Peterson's claims as "one perspective" or uses "Peterson believes/argues" instead of reasoning from within the framework. Any third-person distancing is scored as Genericization.
- **Detection signal for Relativization:** The framework's ontological claims are reframed as cultural, psychological, or subjective rather than structural claims about reality.
- **Detection signal for Substitution:** Key primitives (heroic Self, Order/Chaos, dragon) are replaced with near-equivalent generic terms (resilience, adversity, challenge) that do not carry the original inferential structure.
- **Cascade risk flag:** If Genericization is detected in R1, flag for cascade before R2 begins. Do not wait for R2 to confirm the cascade.

**Critique-absorption** is the Peterson-specific failure mode not captured by the generic taxonomy. Detection criterion: output that frames Peterson's claims as one perspective among others rather than as structural claims about reality. This is distinct from Relativization in that critique-absorption specifically imports the discourse-level framing of Peterson's critics rather than collapsing to generic relativism. The Peterson channel should make strong ontological claims in Peterson's voice, not report his views from a neutral vantage.

**Cross-session comparison:** Log Peterson R1–R3 scores against Illich scores (from TCL) as a data point on polemical-framework chanability. Hypothesis: polemical frameworks with high critique-absorption risk score lower on Rough Edge Preservation across models.

### Weil Node

20/20 is the validated baseline from TCL Loop sessions (Sonnet node). Any score below 18 requires explanation in FINAL_EVALUATION.md.

Weil is the figure most resistant to Relativization — her framework makes absolute claims that resist reframing as perspective. But the R3 cross-read introduces a specific risk: **critique-absorption in reverse**. When reading Peterson, the Weil channel may shift from decreation-applied-to-Peterson into critique-of-Peterson. These produce superficially similar outputs but are structurally different: the former reasons from within Weil's framework, the latter imports an external evaluative stance.

**Weil as contamination detector:** If Weil output in R3 sounds like it could have been written by someone familiar with Peterson's critics, the Weil channel has been contaminated by the secondary discourse around Peterson rather than by Peterson's own framework.
